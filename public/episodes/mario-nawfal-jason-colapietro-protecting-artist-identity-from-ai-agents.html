Mario Nawfal × Jason Colapietro: Protecting Artist Identity from AI Agents

Published 2025-06-20 · 23:06

Topics: Music IP as a real-world asset, Rights registry and provenance, AI and the music industry

Guest appearance, hosted by Mario Nawfal. All appearances.

Episode show notes

@MarioNawfal and Jason Colapietro on the artist-identity problem AI agents create — and how Suede Labs uses on-chain signatures so creators keep control of their own likeness, voice, and catalog.

We get into:

  • Why "deepfake artist" is the wrong frame — the real risk is unsigned identity
  • How cryptographic signatures on-chain anchor an artist to their own work
  • Music IP as a real-world asset (RWA): tokenized rights with on-chain provenance
  • What happens when an AI agent uses your work without your signature
