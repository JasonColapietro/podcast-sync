@MarioNawfal and Jason Colapietro break down the Web3 music stack that Suede Labs is building — the layers musicians have actually been waiting for: rights, royalties, and a real on-chain home for music IP.

We get into:

  • The four layers every musician needs to actually own their work
  • Why the legacy industry's split-pot model destroys creator economics
  • Music IP as a real-world asset (RWA) on Base — with on-chain settlement
  • How the Vault gives artists liquidity now without selling future royalty upside
