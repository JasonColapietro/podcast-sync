Mario Nawfal × Jason Colapietro: AI, Music, and the Future of Culture

Published 2025-09-24 · 19:42

Topics: Music IP as a real-world asset, AI and the music industry

Guest appearance, hosted by Mario Nawfal. All appearances.

Episode show notes

@MarioNawfal sits down with Jason Colapietro (founder, Suede Labs) on what happens at the intersection of AI tooling and the music industry — and why the artists who shape culture should also own the upside.

We get into:

  • Why music has always shaped culture before tech does
  • How AI tools change who gets paid when art moves through the system
  • Music IP as a real-world asset (RWA) — keeping rights with creators, not platforms
  • What an artist-owned, on-chain future actually looks like in practice
