Block × Suede AI: How On-Chain Royalties Actually Work

Published 2025-03-22 · 53:16

Topics: Music IP as a real-world asset, Music royalties and the Vault

Episode show notes

Block AMA with Jason Colapietro on the actual mechanics of on-chain royalties — how settlement, payouts, and creator splits work when the music IP layer lives on-chain instead of in a label spreadsheet.

We get into:

  • Where the royalty lag comes from in the legacy industry (and why it's 18–24 months)
  • How on-chain settlement collapses that to near-instant
  • The Vault: liquidity now for artists, royalty yield for supporters
  • Music IP as a real-world asset (RWA) on Base
