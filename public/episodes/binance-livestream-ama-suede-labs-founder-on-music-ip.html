Episode show notes

Binance Livestream AMA with Jason Colapietro on the music IP stack Suede Labs is building — what's live, where it's heading, and how the pieces fit together for artists who want to actually own their work.

We get into:

  • Why music IP is the next major asset class
  • The Suede Labs registry: court-enforceable proof of authorship on-chain
  • Music IP as a real-world asset (RWA) on Base
  • The Vault: liquidity now for artists, royalty yield for supporters
