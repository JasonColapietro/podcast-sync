Episode show notes

Fity.eth and Jason Colapietro on what actually goes wrong with token launches — the founder-side view of the games, the rugs, and what a launch designed for artists looks like instead.

We get into:

  • The patterns every rug shares (and what gives them away early)
  • Why most artist tokens dump on the people who believed first
  • The Suede Labs launchpad: tax tokens that pay creators in SOL/USDC, not the artist token
  • What a healthy long-term launch actually looks like
