Episode show notes

Jason Colapietro (founder, Suede Labs) sits down with @matthewjmalek to talk about why the music industry's IP layer is structurally broken — and how an artist-owned, on-chain stack actually pays the people doing the work. We get into: • Why songwriters and producers lose rights & revenue at every layer of the stack • Music IP as a real-world asset (RWA): tokenizing rights so creators own and earn from their catalog • Suede Labs' tooling — built so musicians keep ownership and get paid for what they make • How x402 + the Model-Pay-Per-Call (MPP) primitive change who gets paid every time AI uses someone's work • AI as a creator-side tool, not a replacement — agents that work for artists, not around them • Why Base is the right home for the music stack • The Vault: DeFi for music royalties — liquidity now, royalty yield for supporters • Building on Arena, prediction markets, and what crypto-native distribution unlocks for independent creators 🔗 Suede Labs → https://suedeai.ai 🐦 Johnny Suede → https://x.com/johnnysuede 🎙️ Hosted by → https://x.com/matthewjmalek
