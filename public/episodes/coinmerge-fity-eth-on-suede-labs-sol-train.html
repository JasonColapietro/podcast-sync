CoinMerge's Sol Train hosts Fity.eth + Jason Colapietro on what Suede Labs is actually building — the music IP stack, the launchpad design, and what an artist-owned future looks like on Solana and Base.

We get into:

  • Why music infrastructure is the last industry crypto hasn't fixed
  • The launchpad mechanics: tax tokens that pay creators in SOL/USDC
  • Music IP as a real-world asset (RWA) with on-chain provenance
  • What being multi-chain unlocks for independent artists
