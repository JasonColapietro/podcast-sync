AI Suede: build, create, ship.

AI Suede is the podcast feed for Jason Colapietro, also known as Johnny Suede, founder of Suede Labs AI. It carries conversations, AMAs, and clips on creator ownership, music IP as a real-world asset, programmable media, on-chain royalties, and what it actually takes to ship as a solo founder.

Episodes are pulled from the @aisuede YouTube channel and synced to a standard podcast RSS feed so the same conversations are listenable in any podcast app.

For more on the host, see Jason Colapietro's founder bio. For more on the parent company, see About Suede Labs AI.
