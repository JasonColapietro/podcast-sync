Booking a guest spot or an interview

AI Suede carries Jason's own episodes and his guest appearances on other people's podcasts, exchange AMAs and X Spaces. To invite him onto your show, include the programme name, the format (recorded, live or Spaces), the proposed date and the topic. Past appearances are listed on the appearances page, grouped by host.

Press and corrections

For press questions, quotes or a correction to an episode page, email the same address and name the episode URL. Episode pages reproduce the show notes published in the podcast feed, so a correction is made at the source and the page is regenerated from it.
